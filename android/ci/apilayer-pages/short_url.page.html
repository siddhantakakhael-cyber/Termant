What is URL Shortener?

URL Shortener API provides full privacy and ownership on your short url service. Ability to generate thousands of URLs with a powerful API and get all the stats without anyone else having access to it.

Why you should use short URLs?

In social platforms like Twitter where you have character limitations to express yourself, every word counts. You can easily fit more links into your content by using short urls.

When we receive a message, ad or promotion with long URLs that mainly contains a bunch of characters and numbers in it, we have less tendency to trust and click on it. Customized links especially shortened with domain names help us to recognize the brand and trust it easily.

What are features of URL Shortener API?

  • We do not charge any extra fee for using a custom domain in your short url service.
  • If for any reason may you decide to unsubscribe from this service, your short links will still keep on functioning (if otherwise is requested).

Just to clarify… Terms like URL shortener, link shortener, custom short URL or link shrinker are all referring to the same thing. URL Shortener API helps you to transform long URLs to short and customized links.

Easy to track your content performances with URL Shortener API

Recently, more and more URL Shortener services provide analytics tools to track link click rates, referrals, conversion rates and user behaviour. Especially in the social media platforms, those insights help businesses to know their customer base and their interactions better.
