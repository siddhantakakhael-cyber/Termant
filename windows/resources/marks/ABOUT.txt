These Python files are not Termant's own. They are copied, unchanged, from

  https://github.com/guillaumemeyer/watermarks-remover
  folder service/scripts, commit 5a62be6338d2812372b1da440e55c82922f97d53 (1 November 2026)
  MIT licence, Copyright (c) 2026 Guillaume Meyer and contributors (see LICENSE)

They are the part of that project that looks for and removes AI provenance marks
in text and files with Python's standard library only: invisible Unicode in text,
and C2PA / EXIF / XMP / document properties in pictures, PDFs, Office files, audio
and video. `pc marks` (termant-pc.ps1) runs inspect_file.py and clean_file.py.

Left out on purpose: the HTTP server, the statistical-watermark detectors and
scorers, the stylometry score, the pixel-level removers (CtrlRegen, MarkDiffusion),
the "stealer" module, benchmarks and tests.

To update: copy the same eight files from a newer commit, change the commit above,
and run windows/test/pc-test.ps1.
