# Termant for Windows

## 0.9.0
- The PC draws pictures from words: ask "a cat at a window, photo" and the finished picture appears under the answer, in the Pictures folder, and in the web chat on the phone. It costs nothing and needs no account or key; `draw` also works by hand in a tab.
- A click on a picture in the chat opens it in the PC's own viewer.

## 0.8.0
- While the PC works, a small card in the corner says what it is doing, how long, and the newest line a running command printed — the same card the phone shows. It goes away when the answer is done.
- The card is out of the way: it sits in the corner, is not in the taskbar, and does not take the focus from what you do.

## 0.7.0
- The chat of the PC opens in a phone's browser: Settings shows the address and a password that keeps strangers out.
- From that page, a voice can wake it by saying "hey Termant" (an iPhone opens the same page in Safari).
- The agent is told your name, so it can address you.
